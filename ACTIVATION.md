# Activate Full Access

[Illustrated guide](https://edrviewer.yizewu555.workers.dev/activation)

1. Copy the license key from your Lemon Squeezy purchase email.
2. Open License → Activate / manage license… in EDR Viewer 2.18.
3. Paste the key and click Activate while online.

Up to 2 Macs. To move to another Mac, go online and choose Deactivate this Mac on the old Mac before activating the new one. After successful activation, saved authorization works offline without a countdown; online checks resume in the background.
